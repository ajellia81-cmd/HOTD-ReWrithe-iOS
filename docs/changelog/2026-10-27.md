## chandshadow: the chandelier's shadow in the mansion hall

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Chandelier shadow (Chapter 1, entering the hall, arcade models) | the whole chandelier squashed flat in black: a big middle rectangle and two black trapezoids left and right | one rounded blob under the middle, as MAME (snap 0164) |
| Other shadow-pass shadows (creatures, props) | every polygon of the squashed model, coarse texture level | polygons turned away from the shadow light dropped, finer edge (rounder) |

- Cause: HOTD's geometrizer runs without stored normals (MAME geo_parse_nn_ns): a polygon's normal comes from its
  transformed corners. For the shadow pass (a model squashed onto the floor) that makes polygons facing away from

  the squashed matrix, so nothing was culled. Fix: renderer.cpp, arcade models with a squashed matrix only; notes in
  docs/teaser_status/render.md "chandshadow".
